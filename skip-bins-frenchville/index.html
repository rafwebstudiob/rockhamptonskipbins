Skip hire in Frenchville

Frenchville is an established north-side suburb at the foot of Mount Archer, with older homes and leafy yards. Renovations, garden and tree clean-ups and house clear-outs are typical. Older homes may include fibro, so get it checked first.

Sloping blocks

Some streets near the range are steep. Mention slope and access so the operator can plan the drop-off.

Nearest tip

Rockhampton Regional Council's Lakes Creek Road Waste Management Facility takes domestic and commercial waste and is open 7am to 5pm every day, closed Christmas Day. It has a transfer station, a community recycling centre and a reuse shop. Regulated waste, including asbestos loads over 175 kg, must be pre-booked and approved before disposal. Council also runs domestic-only rural facilities at Alton Downs, Bajool, Bouldercombe, Bushley, Gracemere and Mount Morgan. Check the council website for fees and accepted items.

Council rules

A skip wholly on your own land generally doesn't need approval. On the footpath or road, check with Rockhampton Regional Council first. See the permit guide.
